# Lab 4 — Critique non-RIFCC prompts

**Why this matters.** Reviewing other people's prompts is the fastest way to internalize RIFCC.
You can't always rewrite a teammate's prompt for them — but you can spot the missing letter in
their PR description in 10 seconds and unblock the next iteration.

Read `starter/bad_prompts.md`. ≥ 4 prompts violate RIFCC. Find them, rewrite each in `critique.md`.

## Acceptance criteria

- `critique.md` has ≥ 4 rewrites.
- `confidence_score` medium/high.
